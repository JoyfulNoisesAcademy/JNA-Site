About our academy

A joyful beginning for every child

Joyful Noises Academy is a caring creche, daycare and preschool based in Thulare Park, Marapong, Lephalale, South Africa.

We provide a safe and welcoming space where children can learn through play, explore their creativity and develop socially, emotionally and academically.

School requirements

Stationery list

Requirements for Grade R, Grade RR and Grade RRR learners.

Grade R & RR

  • 1 two-quire notebook
  • 1 A4 72-page Irish & Margin notebook
  • 3 HB pencils
  • 1 metal sharpener
  • 1 pack of wax twister crayons
  • 50-page flip file
  • 1 pack of Typek printer paper
  • 12 packets of wipes: 6 for January–June and 6 for July–December

Grade RRR

  • 1 A4 drawing book
